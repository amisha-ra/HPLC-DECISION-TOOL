HPLC Unit Configurations

Important: The physical arrangement and stacking order of modules may vary between HPLC systems. The diagrams below are illustrative and show the different solvent delivery configurations and instrument modules that may comprise a HPLC system.

It is not the module configuration that is important; rather it is the connectivity and direction of the mobile phase flow path through the tubing that determines the functional sequence of the system. This sequence represents the order followed by the mobile phase during HPLC operation and corresponds to the flow path shown in the ‘Instrument Modules’ panel above.
